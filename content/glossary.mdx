# Glossary

42 has its own vocabulary. Here are the words you will hear in your first days.

## Black hole

A countdown, in days, before you lose access to the curriculum. Validating projects adds days to it. Some campuses have replaced it with [milestones](#milestones). See [The Common Core](/wiki/common-core#keeping-your-pace).

## Bocal

The name long used for the school's staff team. Your campus may call it something else.

## Cluster

A room full of computers where students work. You log in to any free computer with your 42 account.

## Common Core

The main curriculum after the Piscine. See [The Common Core](/wiki/common-core).

## Evaluation points

The currency of peer evaluation: you spend them to be evaluated and earn them by evaluating others. Also called *correction points*. Spare points can be donated to the campus [pool](/wiki/common-core#giving-points-back-to-the-pool).

## Exam

A test taken alone, on a school computer, without internet or notes. Exercises come one at a time. See [The Piscine](/wiki/piscine#how-it-works).

## Intra

42's intranet: your profile, projects, evaluations, events and everything official.

## Login

Your 42 username. It is used everywhere: on the intra, in the clusters, in Git repositories.

## Milestones

A deadline system that some campuses use instead of the [black hole](#black-hole): you must reach given stages of the curriculum by given dates.

## Moulinette

The automated grader. It runs tests on your code after the deadline and is famously strict.

## Norminette

The program that checks whether your code follows **the Norm**, 42's coding style: line and function lengths, naming, formatting, allowed constructs. Its source is [on GitHub](https://github.com/42School/norminette).

## Peer evaluation

An evaluation of your project by another student, following an evaluation sheet. You present and explain your code, they test it and ask questions. Honest, careful evaluations are expected on both sides.

## Piscine

The intensive selection month before admission. See [The Piscine](/wiki/piscine).

## Pisciner

A student taking part in the Piscine.

## Rush

A short group project during the Piscine, done in a team over a few days.

## Soldes

French for "sales": on some campuses, an event when evaluations become cheap or free, made possible by points students donated to the pool. See [The Common Core](/wiki/common-core#the-soldes).

## Subject

The PDF describing a project or exercise: what to build, what is allowed and how it will be evaluated. Read it carefully.
