# Admissions

Getting into 42 does not require a diploma, prior coding experience or tuition fees. Admission happens in steps, and the last one is the [Piscine](/wiki/piscine) itself.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Exact requirements (minimum age, dates, number of places) depend on the campus. Check the admissions page of the campus you are applying to.

## 1. Create an account and play the games

You apply on the admissions site of the campus you want to join. After creating an account, you take online games that test **logic and memory**, not programming knowledge. You don't need to know how to code to succeed.

Tips:

- Take them when you are rested and won't be interrupted.
- Read the instructions carefully: understanding the rules is part of the test.

## 2. Check-in

If you pass the games, you are invited to a **check-in**: a short meeting, on site or online depending on the campus, where the school presents how 42 works and you confirm your identity. After it, you can book a place in an upcoming Piscine.

## 3. The Piscine

The Piscine is the real selection: a few intense weeks of projects and exams. Everything you need to know is on [its own page](/wiki/piscine).

## Preparing before you start

You don't need to learn to code beforehand, but a few things make the first days easier:

- **Get comfortable with a keyboard and a terminal.** Moving between folders, creating and editing files.
- **Practise searching for answers.** At 42, `man` pages and search engines are your teachers.
- **Organise your life for the Piscine.** Housing, transport, food and rest: you will spend long days on campus.
