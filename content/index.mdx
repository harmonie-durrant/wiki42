# Introduction

Welcome to **Wiki42**, the unofficial wiki of 42, written by students for current and future students.

> [!NOTE]
>
> This wiki is not affiliated with 42. Rules and schedules change over time and can differ from one campus to another: when in doubt, the intra and your campus staff have the final word.

## What is 42?

42 is a network of computer programming schools, founded in Paris in 2013, with campuses all over the world. It does things differently from most schools:

- **Tuition-free.** Studying at 42 costs nothing.
- **No teachers, no classes.** You learn by working on projects, searching for answers and asking other students.
- **Peer-to-peer.** Your work is evaluated by other students, and you evaluate theirs.
- **Project-based.** Every skill is learned by building something: a library, a shell, a game, a web server…
- **No diploma required.** Anyone old enough can apply; what counts is motivation and the ability to learn.
- **Gamified.** You earn experience, levels and skills as you validate projects.

## How the journey works

1. **[Admissions](/wiki/admissions)**: online logic games, then a check-in to book your place in a Piscine.
2. **[The Piscine](/wiki/piscine)**: an intensive selection month where you learn the basics of C and the shell, and find out whether 42's way of learning suits you.
3. **[The Common Core](/wiki/common-core)**: once admitted, a sequence of projects that takes you from rewriting standard C functions to building a full web application.
4. **After the Common Core**: internships, specialisations and advanced projects, depending on your campus.
